This policy explains how the Magyar Widget iOS app (“the App”) handles information. In short: we do not collect your personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The App does not require an account or login.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress (such as which words you have reviewed and when they are next due) and your settings are stored locally on your device so the App and its widget can work. This data is not sent to us.

If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your backup and is handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or from anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can reset your progress at any time from the App’s settings. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
