Getting started

  1. Open the App and choose a Core deck: 100, 300, or 600 words.
  2. Pick your daily rhythm: how many new words per day.
  3. Add the widget: touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit then Add Widget, search for Magyar, choose Medium or Large, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Glance at a word every time you unlock your iPhone and tap Seen. Spaced recall brings each word back on its own schedule.

Frequently asked questions

The widget isn’t updating.

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule, so a new card may not appear on every single unlock. Open the App once to refresh, make sure Low Power Mode is off, and check that the App is up to date. If it still doesn’t update, remove the widget and add it again, or restart your iPhone.

How does spaced recall work?

Each word returns for review on a schedule. Words you mark Forgot come back sooner; words you mark Remembered come back after longer and longer gaps. Reviews are chosen for you, so you never manage a queue.

Which decks are included?

Three Core decks of high-frequency everyday words: Core 100, Core 300, and Core 600. Each builds on the one before it, so your progress and reviews carry over when you change decks. Every word includes a Hungarian example sentence and an English gloss.

How do I reset my progress?

Use the reset option in the App’s settings. Deleting and reinstalling the App also removes all progress. Resetting cannot be undone.

Do I need an account?

No. The App works without an account, and your progress stays on your device.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on your device. Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one may bring it over.

Does the App collect my data?

No. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.
