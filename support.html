Support

Need help with Magyar Widget? Start here.

Getting started

  1. Open the App and choose a core deck (100, 300, or 600 words).
  2. Review words; ones you find harder come back sooner, and ones you know come back later (spaced recall).
  3. Add the widget: touch and hold your Home Screen, tap + (or Edit → Add Widget), search for Magyar Widget, choose a size, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Glance at a word every time you unlock your iPhone.

FAQ

The widget isn't updating.

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule. Open the App once to refresh, make sure Low Power Mode is off, and check that the App is up to date. If it still doesn't update, remove the widget and add it again, or restart your iPhone.

How do I reset my progress?

Use the reset option in the App's settings. Deleting and reinstalling the App also removes all progress. Resetting cannot be undone.

Do I need an account?

No. The App works without an account and your progress stays on your device.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on your device; restoring from an iPhone backup may bring it over.
