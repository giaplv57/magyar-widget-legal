## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (GitHub Pages). No frameworks, no CDN fonts required for deploy; self-host any display face if used.

## Users

Adults learning everyday Hungarian in Hungary (or preparing to live/travel there)—often busy people who will not open a study app every day, but unlock their iPhone constantly.

## Product Purpose

Magyar Widget helps beginners learn and *remember* high-frequency Hungarian vocabulary through spaced recall and an always-visible Home Screen widget. Success: words stick without carving out another study session.

## Positioning

Spaced recall (Anki-style scheduling) plus effortless Home Screen practice every unlock. Core decks 100 / 300 / 600. Not a generic flashcard list; the widget + recall loop is the differentiator. No account required; progress stays on device.
